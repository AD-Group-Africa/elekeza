# PCEA WORKS + CONNECT — ENTRY STRATEGY BRIEF

September 2026 · Source: PCEA strategy session · Companion: `PORTFOLIO-BRIEF.md`

## Repositioning

PCEA Works + PCEA Connect should be positioned around a sharper institutional opportunity: help PCEA solve **coordination, communication, administration and accountability** across the church — and use **Milele** as a concrete business/operations case rather than treating it as a separate problem.

Market context: PCEA's Communications Department is responsible for keeping the church informed with accurate, clear communication and already spans administration, design and ICT. PCEA also has a substantial institutional footprint (Milele Hotels, schools, hospitals). This is bigger than building a church app.

## 1. The core problem: coordination, not just communication

Information moves through:

```text
General Assembly → Presbyteries → Parishes → Congregations → Ministries → Members
```

while operations also move through:

```text
Church → Institutions → Hotels → Schools → Hospitals → Projects → Suppliers → Staff → Members
```

The result: information scattered across WhatsApp · duplicated records · delayed announcements · unclear responsibility · manual reporting · difficult follow-up · disconnected ministries · institutional information living separately · poor visibility for leadership · admin consuming staff time · communication without a reliable institutional record.

That is precisely where Connect + Works enter.

## 3. Entry Point #1 — Official communication (easiest door)

PCEA already recognizes the need for correct, factual, clear communication and warns members to verify information through official channels.

**Official Announcement Engine:** Head Office / Presbytery / Parish / Ministry announcements · emergency notices · pastoral notices · event notices · circulars · documents · targeted messages.

Instead of "please forward this WhatsApp message":

```text
Official PCEA communication → authenticated source → appropriate audience → delivery → acknowledgement → archive
```

**Killer feature — "Is this official?":** every announcement carries issuing office · authorized person · date · target audience · reference number · supporting document · verification status. Directly addresses the communication credibility problem.

## 4. Entry Point #2 — Church administration (ERP-like)

**Session / Parish administration:** membership · households · elders · districts · ministries · attendance · transfers · baptisms · confirmations · weddings · funerals · pastoral follow-up · meetings · minutes · actions · assignments.

**Leadership dashboard — "What needs attention today?":**

```text
12 pending pastoral follow-ups
 8 unresolved administrative requests
 4 upcoming meetings
 3 overdue actions
 2 announcements awaiting approval
17 new members
 5 visitors requiring follow-up
```

Much more valuable than a church calendar.

## 5. Entry Point #3 — "Who is responsible?"

A major institutional problem: information exists, but responsibility isn't visible. Turn communication into workflow:

```text
Head Office sends directive
  ↓
Presbytery receives
  ↓
Presbytery assigns to parish
  ↓
Parish assigns responsible person
  ↓
Person completes task
  ↓
Evidence uploaded
  ↓
Supervisor verifies
  ↓
Head Office sees completion
```

= **communication + workflow + accountability.**

## 6. Entry Point #4 — Ministry operations

Existing Connect work already provides the ministry architecture (YPCMF, Guild, Youth, Sunday School, Catechism) — don't throw it away. Expand into **Ministry OS**: every ministry gets members · leaders · events · announcements · attendance · activities · projects · documents · finances where appropriate · volunteers · reports · communication. A consistent administrative model across PCEA.

## 7. Entry Point #5 — Meetings → Minutes → Actions

Church administration is heavily meeting-driven:

```text
Meeting → agenda → attendance → minutes → resolutions
        → action items → responsible person → deadline
        → status → evidence → completion
```

Gives PCEA an institutional memory. Instead of searching WhatsApp/email/Word/notebooks/filing cabinets, leadership gets: *What did we decide, who was assigned, and what happened?*

## 8. Entry Point #6 — Document & circular management

PCEA publishes significant material through its website and communications department. Connect becomes the internal source of truth: circulars · policies · minutes · forms · reports · announcements · manuals · ministry documents · General Assembly documents · presbytery documents — with versioning + permissions + search + approval + archive.

## 9. Entry Point #7 — Member communication

**My PCEA:** my parish · my presbytery · my ministry · announcements · events · groups · giving · documents · pastoral requests · registrations · notifications.

Targeted, not broadcast: Youth announcement → Youth members · Sunday School announcement → parents/teachers · Elder meeting → elders · Presbytery circular → relevant leadership.

## 10. PCEA Works — the implementation arm

**Connect = software/platform. Works = implementation + digital transformation + services.** Works can enter before Connect is fully adopted.

Works sells: website development · digital communication · ICT infrastructure · church digitization · data migration · workflow automation · media systems · livestreaming · network deployment · cybersecurity · systems integration · reporting dashboards · digital signage · training · support · software implementation.

**Positioning:** PCEA's Communications Department already covers ICT, administration, printing/design and media. Works positions as an **implementation partner that strengthens those functions — not a competitor.**

## 11–13. The big entry point — MILELE

There is real current institutional tension around Milele Beach Hotel (financial/leadership debates reported publicly). **AD Group must not present itself as having the solution to ownership, governance or financial disputes.** The legitimate technology entry point is the **information and operational visibility problem**.

PCEA owns/operates multiple Milele properties (Nairobi, Beach, Nakuru). Milele Nairobi alone: accommodation, conferences, dining, events/weddings, swimming, bookings — 81 rooms. Effectively a standalone business operation.

**PCEA Works offer — Milele Digital Operations Audit.** Map:

```text
Bookings → Sales → Conference → Accommodation → Restaurant → Events
        → Payments → Expenses → Inventory → Staff → Management
```

Identify: where information is lost · where manual work happens · where revenue leakage can occur · where reporting is delayed · where departments aren't synchronized · where customers drop off · where management lacks real-time visibility.

**Milele OS** (a vertical version of the ERP work):

```text
Bookings → rooms → conference halls → events → restaurant → payments
        → customers → suppliers → inventory → staff → expenses
        → reports → management dashboard
```

Executive dashboard: occupancy · rooms occupied · conference bookings · events · restaurant revenue · room revenue · outstanding invoices · expenses · bookings next 30d.

The conversation changes from "we have an app for PCEA" to **"we can give PCEA management a real-time operational picture of its institutions."**

## 15. AirHop

Several unrelated products use the AirHop name (an offline-first Bluetooth mesh messenger; an AI network-automation company) — clarify which is yours. If it's your own project, the strategic entry point: **AirHop = resilience communication layer.**

- **Connect** handles normal institutional communication.
- **AirHop** handles communication when connectivity is unreliable/unavailable — rural congregations, field missions, conferences, disaster response, large church events, network outages, emergency communication.

**Do not** make AirHop part of the initial PCEA pitch. First prove Connect (communication + administration), then introduce AirHop (resilient/offline).

## 17. Commercial entry strategy — phases

Do NOT walk in saying "we built PCEA Connect, can you buy it?"

| Phase | Move | Notes |
| ----- | ---- | ----- |
| 1 — Diagnostic | **PCEA Digital Operations & Communication Assessment** | Deliverables: communication map, administrative workflow map, data-flow map, systems map, pain points, duplication, reporting gaps, security risks, transformation roadmap. This is PCEA Works. |
| 2 — Quick win | Deploy one visible solution | e.g. PCEA Official Communications Hub, or Events & Circulars Platform. Value demonstrable in weeks. |
| 3 — Connect pilot | **1 Presbytery + 2–5 congregations** | Measure: communication delivery, announcement engagement, administrative turnaround, reporting time, meeting/action completion, member engagement. |
| 4 — Milele | Separate approach | "Let's perform a digital operations assessment of Milele and identify where technology can improve revenue visibility, bookings, reporting and operational coordination." Much easier to sell than "we want to fix Milele." |
| 5 — Group platform | PCEA Digital Operating System | Connect + institutional ERP modules, once evidence exists. |

## 20. The pitch

Not: "We make church apps."

> "We can start small — solve communication and administrative coordination first, measure the results, and then extend the same infrastructure to institutional operations such as Milele."
