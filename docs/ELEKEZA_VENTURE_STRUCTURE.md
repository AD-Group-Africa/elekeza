# ELEKEZA VENTURE STRUCTURE (DRAFT — STRATEGIC EXPLORATION)

**Status: exploration document.** Nothing here is legal, tax or accounting
advice, and no corporate action has been taken. No investment relationship
exists or is implied. All names and structures are options under discussion.

## Current situation (factual)

- Elekeza is built and operated as a project of/within **Afrika Digitalis
  (AD Group)**.
- All IP produced so far sits with whoever owns AD Group's work by default —
  this has not been formally mapped (see open questions).

## The option being explored

```text
AFRIKA DIGITALIS (holding / technology group)
        │  founder or group ownership
        ▼
ELEKEZA (education-technology product company)
        │  dedicated brand, team, cap table
        ▼
PRODUCT  →  Schools (B2B2C)  →  Learners & Guardians
```

### Why a subsidiary may make sense

- **Focused narrative for investors**: education-investors evaluate an
  education company, not a technology group with several projects.
- **Clean cap table for future funding**: a dedicated entity can take
  investment without entangling the parent's other interests.
- **Brand independence**: Elekeza needs its own identity with schools,
  ministries and NGOs.
- **Risk separation**: a failure or liability in one venture does not
  contaminate the other.

### What should stay shared (with AD Group)

- Core engineering leadership and shared infrastructure expertise.
- Design/branding resources where useful.
- Back-office (accounting, legal coordination) until scale demands otherwise.

### What should become independent

- Product IP, codebase and content licences.
- Customer contracts (schools, NGOs).
- Brand and domain assets.
- Any future AI model artefacts or datasets (with privacy controls).

## Open questions (need professional advice)

1. **IP assignment**: is existing Elekeza code/content formally assignable
   to a new entity? Who are the contributors?
2. **Licences**: do all dependencies permit commercial subsidiary use?
3. **Tax & registration**: jurisdiction (Kenya?), structure (Ltd?), timing.
4. **Intercompany agreement**: if AD Group continues to build, on what terms?
5. **Founder equity & vesting** across parent/subsidiary.
6. **Data protection**: learner data is sensitive; entity responsibilities
   under Kenyan Data Protection Act must be assigned clearly.
