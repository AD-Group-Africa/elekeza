# Try Elekeza — Tester Guide

**Elekeza makes learning simpler, more understandable and more accessible.**

## Getting started (2 minutes)

1. Open the link you were given.
2. Click **Register**.
3. Choose your role (Student, Teacher, or Parent/Guardian).
4. Fill in your name, email and a password.
5. You'll land on your dashboard.

Already have an account? Just log in.

## What to try (Student)

1. Pick a lesson from **My Lessons**.
2. Find something hard to understand.
3. Ask the **AI Tutor** to make it simpler.
4. Practice with the **Quizzes**.
5. Open **Exams** and take the exam that's waiting for you.
6. See your score when you submit.
7. Check the bell icon for **notifications**.

## What to try (Teacher)

1. Open **Exams** — create one, publish it.
2. Log in as a student (or ask someone to) and take it.
3. Back in **Exams**, press **Review** on an attempt and mark the written answers.
4. Watch the student's score update.

## What to try (Parent/Guardian)

1. Open **My Child**.
2. Look at progress and exam results.
3. Check **Communication** for updates.
