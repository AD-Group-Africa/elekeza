# TEACHER PILOT GUIDE — Elekeza

Welcome. This guide covers what you can do in Elekeza during the pilot and what
feedback we hope you'll share. Sessions take about 30 minutes the first time.

## Getting in

1. Open the school's Elekeza web address in Chrome/Edge (phone, tablet or laptop).
2. Log in with the account your coordinator gave you.
3. Your dashboard shows your classes, students and shortcuts.

## Daily workflow — attendance (≈2 minutes)

1. Sidebar → **Attendance**.
2. Pick your class and the date (today is pre-selected).
3. Tap each learner's status: **Present / Absent / Late / Excused**.
4. Press **Save register** — the counts confirm what you saved.
5. Tap a learner's **History** any time to see their record.

Saving again the same day updates the register — it never creates duplicates.

## Weekly workflow — classwork & grading

1. Sidebar → **Classwork**.
2. **New assignment**: title, short instructions, due date, points → **Create**.
   Learners see it immediately under their *Assignments* tab.
3. When work comes in, open the assignment → **View submissions**.
4. Enter a score (0 to the assignment's points) and optional feedback → **Save grade**.
5. The learner sees the score and your feedback on their assignment card.

## What we're testing (be honest — you can't break it)

- Can you mark attendance faster than your paper register?
- Can you create and grade classwork without help?
- Does the dashboard tell you who needs attention?
- What's missing that you'd need every week?

## Reporting an issue

Tell your coordinator, or write it in the feedback sheet: **what you tried, what you
expected, what happened** (a screenshot helps). Anything counts — confusion is as
useful to us as bugs.
