# Elekeza — Demo-Day Script (≈12 minutes)

Terminology rule for the whole demo: say **Guardian** for the account role;
**Parent / Caregiver / Older sibling** are *relationships*, not accounts.

## Act 1 — School (SCHOOL_ADMIN) ~2 min

1. On the login page, click **Register your school**; register "Demo School"
   live (name + admin email + password). Point out: this creates a
   **School Admin** — institution-scoped — *not* a platform admin.
2. In the school dashboard: open **Import students**, download the CSV template,
   show the `guardianRelationship` column (Parent / Caregiver / Older sibling /
   Legal guardian / Other) — "the school decides who is an authorized adult."
3. Show the learners roster.

**Fallback:** if live registration is not desired, log in with a pre-registered
school admin created earlier the same morning (runbook §Pre-demo).

## Act 2 — Teacher ~3 min

Log in as `teacher@elekeza.app` / `teacher123`.

1. Students list → open **Juma Ali** → Learning-support panel:
   preferences ("Step-by-step — chosen by you"), observed signals, no medical labels.
2. Assign "The Water Cycle" to Juma (one click) if not already assigned.
3. Show the assignment in the assignments list.

## Act 3 — Learner ~3 min

Log in as `student@elekeza.app` / `student123`.

1. **How I Learn** page: set Step-by-step, Roomier layout, Plenty of examples.
   "Every learner gets a profile — with or without a disability."
2. Open the assigned lesson → **My usual style** → adapted step-by-step view;
   show "Original" toggle, "More detail", 👍 feedback.
3. Open the seeded quiz, answer one question.

## Act 4 — Guardians ~3 min (the differentiator)

Say: *"Guardian doesn't necessarily mean parent. The school authorizes a parent,
caregiver, older sibling, or another responsible adult — each with their own
account, linked to the learner."*

1. Log in as `parent@elekeza.app` / `parent123` → Guardian Dashboard:
   Juma's progress, quiz summary, plain-language learning card
   ("adjusts how lessons are presented… does not change the curriculum or
   diagnose a condition").
2. Log out → `sibling@elekeza.app` / `sibling123` → same learner, tab shows
   **Older sibling**.
3. Log out → `caregiver@elekeza.app` / `caregiver123` → same learner,
   labeled **Caregiver**.
4. Punchline: three adults, one learner, one account role, three relationship
   types — and none of them can see any other learner.

## Act 5 — School visibility ~1 min

Back as the Act-1 school admin: school-level reports/analytics; learner
progress visible; end on "one platform for the whole school."

## Closing line

"Elekeza personalizes the way education is experienced by every learner —
and gives every adult around the learner the right, scoped view."
