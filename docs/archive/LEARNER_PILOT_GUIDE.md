# LEARNER PILOT GUIDE — Elekeza

Hi! This is your quick guide to Elekeza.

## Getting started

1. Open Elekeza on your school tablet, phone or computer.
2. Type your username and password, then press **Login**.
3. Your home page greets you and shows your level and stars.

## Every day

- **Continue learning** — opens the lesson you were working on. Use **Next section**
  to move through it at your own pace.
- **My Assignments** — work your teacher set for you:
  - Press **Open and answer**, write your answer, press **Submit**.
  - Finished but want to do better? Press **Improve my answer** and submit again.
    Your teacher always sees your best one.
  - When your teacher grades it, you'll see your score and their feedback.
- **Quizzes / Exams** — your teacher will tell you when to open these.

## If something is hard

- Try the **How I Learn** page — you can change text size, spacing and colours.
- **Read aloud** buttons read the lesson to you.
- Stuck? Ask your teacher. It's never your fault when something breaks —
  that's what we want to hear about!

## Tell us what you think

Your teacher has a feedback sheet. The most helpful things to say:

- What did you press first, and why?
- Was anything confusing or too small to read?
- What do you wish Elekeza could do?
