# GUARDIAN PILOT GUIDE — Elekeza

Welcome. This guide is for parents and caregivers using Elekeza during the pilot.
First session takes about 20 minutes.

## Getting in

1. Open Elekeza in your phone or computer browser.
2. Log in with the phone-number account your school registered (or the email your
   coordinator gave you).
3. The **Guardian Dashboard** lists your children.

## Understanding your child's day — "Today at a glance"

Open a child's page. The **Today at a glance** card shows, from real school records:

- **Lessons done** — what they completed, and what's still to go
- **Attendance** — their rate, and whether they were marked today
- **Classwork** — what's coming up, what's past due, what's done ✓
- **Fees balance** — what the school records as outstanding (or "all clear")

Below the card: completed lessons, average score, recent quizzes and progress history.

## Fees and receipts

Sidebar → **Fees** shows each charge, what has been paid, and the balance.
During the pilot, the M-Pesa button runs in **test mode** — you'll see
"Test mode: payment request created locally". No real money moves. Receipts open
from the payment history.

## How you can support learning (no teaching required)

- Open **My Lessons**-style links from your child's page and read a lesson together.
- Ask about a "past due" item on the digest card — then help them open and finish it.
- Read teacher feedback on graded work with your child; praise specifics.

## What we're testing

- Does the daily summary make sense at a glance — not too much, not too little?
- Can you find what your child should do next?
- Is the fee balance clear?
- Anything confusing on your phone's small screen?

## Reporting an issue

Tell the school coordinator, or note: **what you tried, what you expected, what
happened**. Confusion is exactly what we want to learn about — please report it.
